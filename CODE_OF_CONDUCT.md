# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Iris community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers at
**marcovito.dev@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
